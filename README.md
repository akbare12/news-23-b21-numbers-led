# The World Today — Monday 23 November 2026

One image summing up the world's news of Monday 23 November 2026, led by the
day's giant numbers: the Kospi's -3.3% AI rout, the US–China power gap at a
record-low 5.3, 11,762 Ebola cases in the DRC, a 10T-won Seoul rescue fund, a
2% billionaire-tax plan, Skubal's $450M Mets deal, 165 Russian missiles in a
week, 12,000 cleared in Manchester, 264 kg of explosives seized in Lebanon,
and Anthropic's $107.3B IPO.

31 stories across 6 regions, with the three hero stories drawn as scenes
(AI rout in Asia, Guterres urging AI rules at Bridgetown, the US lead over
China at a record low), a world map with 31 numbered pins, and a DATE CHECK
box that keeps stories from other days out of the picture.

`main.py` copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (stdlib only).
`draw.py` draws the image with PIL. Scenes are drawn illustrations, not
photographs.
